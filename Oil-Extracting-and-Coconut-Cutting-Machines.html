Mechmark Industries offers a mini commercial oil processing unit, including updated versions of traditional coconut expeller machines for oil extraction. These machines are smaller than traditional coconut expeller machines, making them suitable for smaller spaces while maintaining efficiency. They are equipped with a heater for optimal oil extraction and are designed to keep the oil mill hygienic and clean. These machines can extract oil from various seeds, providing versatility for different oil processing needs. Mechmark Industries also provides a machine specifically designed for cutting dried coconuts, further enhancing their product range for efficient food processing solutions.
