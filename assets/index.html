About Us

Precision in Machinery, Excellence in Quality!

  • Mechmark
  • Mission
  • Vision

Mechmark Industries, established in 2010, specializes in manufacturing food processing machineries. Headquartered in Kozhikode, Kerala, India, our precise location is at Puthoor Post, just 30 km from the city. We provide genuine service across India and partially worldwide. At Mechmark Industries, we take pride in our state-of-the-art factory where every machine is meticulously manufactured. With a commitment to excellence, we strive to deliver top-quality products to meet the diverse needs of our customers globally.

Read More

Our mission at Mechmark Industries is to promote the availability of healthy and organic food products in society through the use of our advanced food processing machineries. We are committed to manufacturing high-quality machines that enable food producers to maintain the nutritional value and purity of their products. By providing reliable and efficient equipment, we aim to contribute to the production of safe and healthy food items that benefit individuals and communities. Through innovation and dedication, we strive to be a driving force in the promotion of healthy living and sustainable food practices.

Read More

Our vision at Mechmark Industries is to be a global leader in the manufacturing of food processing machineries, renowned for our commitment to excellence and innovation. We envision a future where our machines play a pivotal role in ensuring the availability of healthy and organic food products worldwide. By continuously enhancing our products and services, we aim to exceed customer expectations and contribute significantly to the advancement of the food processing industry. Through sustainable practices and a focus on customer satisfaction, we strive to create a positive impact on society and the environment.
